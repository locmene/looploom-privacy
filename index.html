Looploom · Amit M

Privacy policy

Effective date: 3 October 2026

This policy explains how the Looploom Android game handles information. Looploom is made by Amit M. It works offline, has no account system, and does not send your game progress to the developer.

Information stored on your device

Looploom saves your high scores, daily scores, run totals, cosmetic currency and palette choices, sound and display preferences, and whether you have seen the tutorial. This information stays in the app’s private storage on your device. It is not uploaded to an Amit M server. Clear the app’s storage or uninstall the game to remove these local saves.

Daily challenges use the current UTC date on the device. The game does not send your score, challenge results, or a leaderboard entry to us.

Optional advertising and Google

Some versions of Looploom may offer an optional rewarded advertisement in exchange for cosmetic in-game currency. An ad is shown only after you choose the ad option. Gameplay and ordinary rewards do not require watching ads. Ads need an internet connection; offline play remains available.

When advertising is enabled in the version you use, Looploom uses Google Mobile Ads and Google’s consent tools. When that SDK is initialized to request ads, Google says it may automatically collect and share your device’s IP address (which can indicate general location), product interactions such as app launches, taps and video views, app and SDK diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID and app set ID. Google processes this information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention, and encrypts the data it collects in transit using TLS. We do not receive your Advertising ID or use it for our own analytics.

Whether an ad is personalized depends on your consent, applicable settings, and Google’s ad systems. You can change the consent choices presented by Google in the game’s optional ads and privacy screen when available, and use your Android privacy and advertising settings to manage your device’s Advertising ID. Google describes its own data practices in its Privacy Policy and advertising information.

Advertising and consent SDKs may communicate with Google when advertising is enabled and you open the optional ads screen. Those network communications do not host or store your game progress, and Looploom does not require a game server.

Sharing a weave

If you choose “Share your weave,” the game creates an image containing the weave and score and opens Android’s share menu. The image and accompanying text are passed to the app or service you select. We do not receive a copy. The selected app or service handles anything you share according to its own privacy policy.

Information we do not request

Looploom has no sign-in, asks for no name or age, and does not request location, contacts, microphone, or photos. The game has no developer-run analytics service, cloud save, or online leaderboard. The advertising SDK’s collection, when enabled, is described above.

Children

Looploom is a general-audience game and is not specifically designed for children. The game does not ask for or determine a player’s age. The publisher should select the intended audience and advertising settings truthfully in Google Play Console; a release intended for children may require a different configuration and additional disclosures.

Retention, deletion, and security

We retain no copy of your local game progress. It remains in the app’s private device storage until you clear the app’s data or uninstall it. If Google Mobile Ads is enabled, Google handles information collected by its SDK under Google’s retention, deletion, security, and privacy practices. Use Google’s privacy controls and your device settings to manage those choices; contact Google for requests about information Google processes.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if the game’s information practices change. The effective date at the top shows when this version was last updated.
